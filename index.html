About This App

MobiFixer is a comprehensive business management solution designed specifically for mobile repair shops. It helps shop owners and technicians streamline their daily operations, manage customers efficiently, and organize service records.

Key Features

  • Customer & Service Management
  • Real-time Job Tracking
  • Inventory Management System
  • Income & Expense Tracking
  • Cloud Backup & Sync
  • Customizable Themes
  • Offline-First Architecture
  • Mobile-Optimized UI
